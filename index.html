Questions

Straight answers

Is the preview really free?

Yes. We design your preview before you pay anything. If you don't want it, you owe nothing.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. We'll look at your current site, tell you plainly what's working and what isn't, and show you a better version.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo if you have one, some photos, your services and prices, and your hours. If you don't have photos yet, we'll help.

Are there monthly fees?

No. The build is paid once. Hosting a simple site is usually free or a few dollars a month. The $60 care plan is optional.

How long does it take?

Your free preview takes a few days. Once you say yes, your site is usually live in 1 to 3 weeks, depending on the plan.
