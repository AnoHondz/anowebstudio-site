Privacy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Short and plain: we only collect what you send us, we use it to reply to you and make your free preview, and we never sell it.

Who we are

Ano Web Studio designs websites for small businesses. If you have any question about your details, email anotida.h@anowebstudio.com.

What we collect

Only what you type into the form on our website:

  • your name and business name
  • your email address
  • your current website, if you give one
  • the plan you're interested in, and your message

We don't use tracking cookies or advertising trackers on this site.

What we use it for

  • To reply to you and arrange a call if you want one.
  • To make your free preview. If you give us your current website, we look at its public pages (your services, hours, photos) so the preview fits your business.
  • To build your website if you decide to go ahead.

We don't add you to a mailing list and we never sell or share your details for marketing.

Who helps us

A few trusted services handle your details on our behalf:

  • Netlify hosts this website and receives the form.
  • Neo provides our email, where your message arrives.
  • Google Fonts and public code libraries load parts of this page, so they can see your device's IP address, as with any website.

How long we keep it

We keep your enquiry only as long as we need it to talk to you and work on your website. If we don't end up working together, you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the details we hold about you, or to stop emailing you. Just reply to any email from us or write to anotida.h@anowebstudio.com, and we'll do it promptly.

Changes

If we change how we handle your details, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
